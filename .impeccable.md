## Design Context

### Users
FGA-track gemmology students and self-taught hobbyists working through identification curriculum. They sit with the article open during a focused study block (often evenings, often after-work fatigue). They want signal density without noise. Many will have a 10× loupe or a refractometer next to the screen — the screen is one instrument among many, not the centre of attention. A non-trivial fraction is colour-vision-impaired (gemmology disproportionately attracts people who train themselves around colour weakness).

### Brand Personality
**Scientifically clean and intriguing.** Three-word distillation: *precise, considered, alive*. The site is reference material that should feel handled — like a well-thumbed mineralogy textbook, the printed legend on a microscope eyepiece, or the engraved scale on a gemmologist's polariscope. Clean does not mean empty; intrigue does not mean decoration. The page must not feel dead.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Typographic-led, not graphic-led.** Hierarchy comes from weight, size, leading, and a controlled accent palette — never from drop shadows, gradient fills, or icon decoration.
- **Quiet accent, sharp rhythm.** The existing `crystal:` (sky-blue) palette is the single chromatic anchor. `gem:` tokens are reserved for mineral-data contexts. Slate is the body neutral.
- **Instrument-label moments.** Where the interface acts (correct/wrong feedback, "Quick check" header, badges), it should read like an etched marking on a brass scale — confident, small, definite. Avoid SaaS-style coloured pills with rounded everything.
- **Light-first, dark-fluent.** Light is the default reading surface; dark mode must be a true mirror, not a partial inversion.

### Anti-references
- **Not Duolingo or gamified-flashcard energy.** No bouncing, no streak fire, no celebration glitter.
- **Not generic admin/SaaS.** No left-stripe-coloured callouts, no gradient hero text, no purple-to-blue accent gradients, no Inter, no Syne.
- **Not a "lab tech demo."** No glowing neon-on-near-black, no monospace as decoration. Monospace is reserved for actual readouts (counters, codes, formulae).
- **Not flat-card-grid templates.** Cards exist; they should not feel mass-produced.

### Theme & Constraints
- **Theme**: light + dark, both must be first-class. Dark is for late-evening study and must remain readable without reaching for a brightness slider.
- **Stack**: Tailwind v3 with `crystal:` and `gem:` palette extensions in `tailwind.config.mjs`; dark mode via `class` strategy on `<html>`.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.2 AA non-negotiable; the site was just remediated. Maintain ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on body text, ≥ 3:1 on large/non-text. Do not regress.
- **Motion**: subtle, exponential easing (ease-out-quart/quint), under 200ms for state changes. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **No emoji** in product UI. Icons come from Lucide React when needed.

### One-line aesthetic vow
*Restrained, precise, with one moment per surface that makes the eye pause.*
